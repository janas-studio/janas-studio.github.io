Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JANAS Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use any of our mobile games and apps distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play (each, an "App"). Not every App offers every feature described here (such as ads, ad measurement, saving progress with Game Center or Google Play Games, saving images, receiving app settings, or usage analytics). Each App handles only the information needed for the features it actually provides. You can also check what each App handles in its App Privacy section (App Store) or Data safety section (Google Play). If we add a new feature or third-party service, we will update this policy before it takes effect.

If you have any questions, please contact us at pioneer.janas@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in (signing in to Game Center or Google Play Games is optional), and we do not ask you for your name, phone number, address, or other information that directly identifies you.

1.1 Information collected automatically for advertising

Apps that display ads show them through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google may automatically collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (Apple IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language settings)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)

1.2 Game progress saved with Game Center and iCloud (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android)

In Apps that support Apple Game Center, if you are signed in, your game progress (for example, the stage you have reached) is stored in Game Center's saved games in your Apple account (iCloud). If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is also stored in iCloud key-value storage in your Apple account. In Apps that support Google Play Games Services on Android, if you are signed in to Play Games, your game progress is stored in Play Games saved games in your Google account.

We do not collect or send your Game Center, Apple, Play Games, or Google account information (such as your nickname, player ID, or email address), and we do not store your progress on our own servers. If you do not sign in, the App keeps your progress only on your device.

1.3 Information stored only on your device

Your game progress, settings (such as music, sound effects, vibration, and language), tutorial history, and whether review requests or update notices have been shown are stored on your device and are not sent to us. If you use Game Center or iCloud, or Google Play Games, in an App that supports them, your progress is also saved to Apple or Google as described in Section 1.2. On Android, your progress and settings stored on the device may be included in the Google backup you have turned on in your device settings and restored when you reinstall the App or switch devices; this backup is kept in your Google account and we cannot access it. Apps that use usage analytics also send the usage information described in Section 1.7 to Google. Apps that we promote with ads on Meta also send the information described in Section 1.8 to Meta.

1.4 Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

1.5 Photo library

If an App lets you save images to your photo library, it only asks for permission to add photos. It does not read your existing photos or send them to us.

1.6 App settings received with Google Firebase

Apps that use Google Firebase Remote Config receive settings from Google when they start, such as notices about a new version or new content. When settings are requested, Google may automatically collect:

  • A Firebase installation ID (a random identifier that distinguishes an installation of the App)
  • App version, operating system type and version, and language and country settings

We use this only to operate the App (for example, to show update notices). We do not use it to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

1.7 Usage analytics with Google Firebase

Apps that use Google Analytics for Firebase collect usage statistics to understand how the App is used and to improve it (for example, to adjust level difficulty and how often ads appear). Google may automatically collect:

  • An app instance ID (a random identifier that distinguishes an installation of the App)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language and country settings) and app version
  • App usage information (number of launches and time spent, levels started and completed, time and moves taken, and whether ads were watched)
  • IP address, used to estimate approximate location at the country or region level
  • Your advertising identifier (on iOS only if you have allowed tracking; on Android unless you have deleted your advertising ID)

We use this only to operate and improve the App. We do not use it to identify you, we do not link it to your name or contact details, and we do not use it to personalize ads. Test builds under development do not collect usage information.

1.8 Ad measurement with Meta

Apps that we promote with ads on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) include the Meta SDK (App Events). It starts only after you respond to the ad consent prompts (the tracking prompt on iOS, and consent in the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland), and it sends nothing in those regions if you do not give consent. Meta may automatically collect:

  • An anonymous ID generated by the Meta SDK (a random identifier that distinguishes an installation of the App)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language and time zone settings) and app version
  • App events: app installs and launches, and levels completed
  • IP address
  • Your advertising identifier (on iOS only if you have allowed tracking; on Android unless you have deleted your advertising ID)

We use this only to see whether our ads lead to installs and play, and Meta uses it to decide who to show our ads to. We do not send your name or contact details to Meta. If you have not allowed tracking on iOS, or have deleted your advertising ID on Android, your advertising identifier is not sent, and Meta uses the information only in aggregated form for ad measurement. Test builds under development do not send this information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Show ads, provide personalized ads where you have allowed it, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud
  • Save your game progress and let you continue on another device (Apps that support Game Center and iCloud, or Google Play Games)
  • Respond to your inquiries and fix problems
  • Deliver app settings such as update and new-content notices (Apps that use Firebase)
  • Understand how the App is used and improve it, such as adjusting level difficulty and ad frequency (Apps that use Firebase analytics)
  • Measure whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to installs and play, and help Meta show our ads to people likely to enjoy the App (Apps that we promote with Meta ads)

4. International Data Transfers

Google, Apple, and Meta may process information on servers located in the United States and other countries. These transfers occur over the network while you use the App (for example, when an ad is loaded, when progress is saved, when app settings are received, or when usage information collected on your device is sent periodically), and are protected by the safeguards described in each provider's privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Advertising information
    • Retention: As described in Google's privacy policy
  • Game Center and iCloud progress
    • Retention: As described in Apple's privacy policy; you can delete it in your iCloud settings
  • Google Play Games progress
    • Retention: As described in Google's privacy policy; you can delete it in the Google Play Games app settings
  • Email inquiries
    • Retention: Deleted within 1 year after your inquiry is resolved
  • App settings information (Firebase)
    • Retention: As described in Google's privacy policy
  • Usage analytics information (Firebase)
    • Retention: Usage events are automatically deleted 2 months after collection, and user-level information such as the app instance ID 14 months after your last use (based on the Google Analytics data retention settings)
  • Ad measurement information (Meta)
    • Retention: As described in Meta's privacy policy
  • Information stored on your device
    • Retention: Until you delete the App

6. Your Choices About Ads and Tracking

  • iOS: The App asks for your permission to track before personalized ads are shown. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland: We ask for your consent before using your information for personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time, including from Privacy options in the App's settings where available.
  • Usage analytics: Usage information is not sent while your device is offline. In the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland, collection is limited if you do not give consent. Refusing does not affect your use of the App.
  • Ad measurement (Meta): If you do not allow tracking (iOS) or delete your advertising ID (Android), your advertising identifier is not sent to Meta. In the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland, nothing is sent to Meta if you do not give consent.

If you opt out, you can still use the App normally, but you may see non-personalized ads.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at pioneer.janas@gmail.com. We will respond without undue delay.

Because we do not store information that identifies you in the App, you can also manage your information directly:

  • Delete the App to remove information stored on your device.
  • Turn off tracking (iOS) or reset or delete your advertising ID (Android) in your device settings (Section 6).
  • Manage Game Center saved data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud on your iPhone.
  • Delete Google Play Games saved data in the settings of the Google Play Games app on Android.

EEA and UK users: We rely on your consent for personalized advertising and on our legitimate interests to provide and improve the App. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents: We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising identifiers may be shared with Google for personalized (cross-context behavioral) advertising, and with Meta to measure and optimize our ads; you can opt out using the device settings described in Section 6.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages but is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, such as 14 in Korea or 16 in some EU countries). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We do not operate our own servers and do not store your game progress ourselves. Email inquiries are handled only in a secured email account and deleted once they are no longer needed.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page at least 7 days before they take effect, or at least 30 days in advance for significant changes affecting your rights.
